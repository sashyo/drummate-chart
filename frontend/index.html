Terms & takedown · Loop: press Loop, then tap the first bar and the last bar (works with a mouse or a finger). Shortcut: click a bar, then shift-click the last one. The looped bars turn yellow. Simple reduces the chart to a plain kick / snare / hi-hat groove on an 8th-note grid — ideal for learning the song before tackling the full part. Detail hides the quietest hits when a chart comes out too busy. Both change what you see and print, not the MIDI. Automatic transcription is a starting point: turn on Edit mode to fix anything it misheard.
