Terms & takedown

DrumMate Chart writes drum notation from audio so drummers can practise. It is free, run by one person, and it works from audio you provide.

What you may use it with

  • Audio you own or have the right to use for this purpose (your own recordings, purchased tracks for your personal practice, material you are licensed to use).
  • The site does not fetch from YouTube or other streaming services. A link must point straight to an audio file.
  • You are responsible for the audio you upload or link. Don't use this to redistribute music.

What happens to your audio

  • It is used only to make your chart: the drums are separated from the mix, the hits are detected, and notation is written.
  • The separated drum track and the drums-removed backing are available to you for practice inside the app for a short time, and exist only while you have the chart open and are deleted when you leave (or after 20 minutes without activity). The source audio you uploaded or linked, and every intermediate file, is deleted the moment the chart is finished.
  • What is kept: the chart itself (score.json), the MIDI and MusicXML exports, and the Clone Hero chart package (notation only — no audio). These let your chart link keep working.
  • No accounts, no personal data. Usage is counted with hashed, unidentifiable numbers.

Takedown

If you hold rights in a recording or composition and believe a chart on this site should be removed, send the chart link and a short description to the contact address on drummate.app. It will be taken down promptly, no argument.

No warranty

The notation is generated automatically and will contain mistakes. It is a practice aid, not a published transcription.
